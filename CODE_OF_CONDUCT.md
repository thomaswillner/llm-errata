# Code of Conduct

LLM Errata exists to be challenged. Its central artifact is a bounded claim that
invites refutation, so the conduct that matters most here is how people handle
disagreement about evidence.

## Expected behaviour

- Argue about evidence, not about people.
- State what you actually checked, when, and how. Distinguish what a source says
  from what you infer from it.
- Accept a correction when the evidence supports it. Withdrawing or narrowing a
  claim is the intended outcome of this project, not a loss.
- Assume that a person who disagrees with you has read something you have not.
- Respect the licensing, confidentiality, and personal-data boundaries of the
  material you cite.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, demeaning comments, or discrimination of any
  kind.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Misrepresenting a source, fabricating a citation, or presenting a generated
  summary as a primary source.
- Arguing a prior-art collision away on rhetoric after the evidence has been
  established.
- Sustained disruption of technical discussion.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
other space where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's private reporting
channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are read by the
maintainer, who will respond as promptly as an independent project allows.

Include what happened, where, and when. If you are reporting on behalf of
someone else, say so.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this
document, and may block a participant from the repository. Enforcement decisions
are made by the maintainer and are not themselves a subject for public debate in
the repository.

Factual disagreements, prior-art challenges, and design objections are never
conduct violations. Handle them through
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) instead.
